Advisory Services

Walking with you,
every step of the way.

From the first conversation about your rights, to the day your trust is running strong and your community is governing its own future — Purple Wombat is with you. Seven services, one commitment: communities know best.

Our Seven Services

End-to-end support for the native title settlement process.

01
Foundation

Native Title Policy Navigation

Before any community makes a decision about opting into the new NSW Government policy, they need to understand it — properly, in plain language, in a culturally safe way. That's where we start. Purple Wombat provides community-led briefings, facilitated discussions and ongoing support so your community can make informed decisions about your options. We don't tell you what to decide. We make sure you have everything you need to decide for yourself.

  • Plain-language briefings on the new NSW Government policy and its implications for your community
  • Facilitated community consultations that centre community voices
  • Support in understanding and responding to government consultation processes
  • Honest assessment of the pros and cons of opting in versus litigating
  • Ongoing engagement as the policy evolves
02
Legal & Structural

Trust Establishment

Setting up a legally compliant charitable trust is one of the most consequential steps in the process. A well-structured trust protects the community's compensation for generations. A poorly structured one can create governance problems, regulatory issues, and community conflict for decades. Purple Wombat provides end-to-end support to get it right from the start.

  • Drafting and reviewing Trust Deeds in line with NSW Government trust governance principles — perpetuity, transparency, governance accountability, transition to RNTBC control
  • ACNC (Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission) registration support
  • Selecting and briefing professional trustees who align with community values
  • Designing the transition pathway from professional trustee to RNTBC-appointed trustee
  • Reviewing draft deeds against community aspirations before signing
03
Financial

Investment Policy & Fund Management Advisory

The trust needs to grow — at or above inflation — in perpetuity. That requires a clear investment strategy, appropriate asset allocation and robust oversight. Purple Wombat supports communities to develop an Investment Policy Statement that reflects both the financial requirements and the community's values, and to establish frameworks that hold trustees accountable for performance.

  • Developing an Investment Policy Statement (IPS) tailored to the community's goals
  • Asset allocation advice (the Government model assumes a blended 70% conservative / 30% growth approach — we help communities understand whether this is right for them)
  • Trustee accountability and reporting frameworks
  • Independent investment performance review mechanisms
  • Ongoing oversight support as market conditions change
04
Governance

RNTBC Governance & Capacity Building

The transition from professional trustee to community-controlled governance is one of the most important journeys an RNTBC undertakes. For that transition to succeed, the RNTBC needs to build genuine operational capacity — not just in governance knowledge, but in financial management, strategic planning and people. Our goal is not to create dependency on Purple Wombat — it is to build the governance, financial and strategic capacity of each community and RNTBC so they thrive independently.

  • Board governance training tailored to RNTBC structures and CATSI Act obligations
  • Financial management and compliance support
  • Strategic planning aligned to community aspirations
  • Staffing structure advice — CEO, CFO, Cultural Heritage Officer, Rangers, and other roles
  • Cultural governance integration — ensuring decision-making reflects cultural protocols
05
Community

Community Distribution Frameworks

Trust income flows to community. But how it flows — who gets what, for what purposes, through what processes — matters enormously. Without a clear distribution framework, even well-funded trusts can become sources of community conflict. Purple Wombat supports communities to develop distribution policies that reflect their aspirations and values, and that are fair, transparent and culturally grounded.

  • Community benefit distributions to native title holders — structure, frequency and eligibility
  • Cultural, social and economic program funding frameworks
  • Land and water acquisition strategies
  • Ranger and Country management program funding models
  • Intergenerational wealth frameworks — balancing present need with future capital
06
Legal

ILUA Support & Negotiation Advisory

An Indigenous Land Use Agreement is one of the most complex and consequential documents a native title group will ever sign. Purple Wombat provides plain-language guidance on what an ILUA means — what rights you're agreeing to constrain, what benefits you should be seeking, and what protections are non-negotiable. We don't replace your lawyers, but we make sure the community understands what's on the table before anything is agreed.

  • Plain-language ILUA briefings for community members and decision-makers
  • Guidance on what to negotiate for — land transfers, heritage protections, employment opportunities, commercial arrangements
  • Review of draft ILUAs to identify issues and gaps before signing
  • Support in developing the community's negotiating position
  • Liaison support between the community and government negotiators
07
Advocacy

Compensation Calculation Review

When the Government presents a compensation offer, communities have the right to scrutinise how it was calculated. The inputs — claim size, group size, aspirations budget — directly determine the lump sum offered. Purple Wombat assists communities to understand and challenge the calculation where appropriate, and supports independent assessment processes where disputes arise. You have rights. We help you exercise them.

  • Plain-language explanation of how the Government calculated the compensation offer
  • Critical analysis of the inputs: claim size assumptions, group size data, aspirations budget
  • Identification of items that may have been omitted or undervalued
  • Support in preparing a community response to the Government's offer
  • Coordination of independent assessment processes where required
Our Approach

We walk alongside you — we don't lead you.

Every community is different. Every situation is unique. Purple Wombat does not arrive with pre-packaged solutions. We start by listening — understanding your community's history, your aspirations, your relationships and your concerns.

We work at the community's pace, in a culturally appropriate and trauma-informed way. Our engagement is designed around free, prior and informed consent — not project timelines or government deadlines.

"This is your money, your future, your decision. We're here to make sure you have everything you need to make the right calls — not to make them for you."

Our Clients

We work with Aboriginal communities and RNTBCs across NSW.

Primary Clients

Aboriginal Communities & Native Title Groups

Communities with existing consent determinations who have not yet settled compensation. Native title groups who have lodged (or are about to lodge) a native title claim that meets the credible evidence standard. RNTBCs seeking to build the capacity to manage trust funds and deliver on community aspirations.

Also

Community Members & Partner Organisations

Individual community members seeking to understand how the policy affects them and what distributions they may be entitled to. Community-controlled organisations that partner with RNTBCs on cultural, social and economic programs — and who want to understand how trust funding can support their work.
