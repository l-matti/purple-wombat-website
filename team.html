Leadership
Founder & Director

Victor Perry

Founder & Director — Purple Wombat

Victor Perry founded Purple Wombat from a clear-eyed understanding of both the complexity of the native title system and the profound opportunity it represents for Aboriginal communities across NSW. With deep roots in community and a career spanning Aboriginal governance, cultural heritage, and community development, Victor brings the cultural authority and the technical knowledge that this work demands.

Victor's work has been shaped by years of walking alongside communities through complex processes — from heritage negotiations to governance reform — always with the principle that communities are the experts on their own lives and futures. Purple Wombat reflects that conviction in everything it does.

As the NSW Government's proposed new native title policy creates one of the most significant opportunities in the state's native title history, Victor is focused on ensuring that Aboriginal communities have access to the trusted, culturally safe advisory support they need to seize it — on their own terms, at their own pace.

"This is your money, your future, your decision. We're here to make sure you have everything you need to make the right calls — not to make them for you."

Chief Operating Officer

Sven Pekarsky

Chief Operating Officer — Purple Wombat

Sven Pekarsky brings to Purple Wombat a distinguished career at the intersection of government policy, complex program delivery and high-stakes commercial negotiation. His experience spans senior advisory roles across federal and state government, where he has led the design and implementation of major policy frameworks and large-scale programs — translating policy intent into outcomes that work on the ground.

Sven's commercial acumen is matched by his ability to navigate the complex stakeholder environments that characterise government-community engagement. He has negotiated on behalf of both public and private sector clients in environments requiring precision, patience and trust — qualities that are essential in native title and Indigenous land use work, where the stakes for communities are generational.

As Chief Operating Officer, Sven leads Purple Wombat's operational strategy, client engagement frameworks and the coordination of specialist advisory services — ensuring that every community we work with receives a seamless, high-quality experience from first contact through to long-term governance support.

"Good policy means nothing if it doesn't translate into real outcomes for real people. My work has always been about closing that gap — making sure the machinery of government and commerce actually serves the people it's designed to."

Our Advisory Capability

The disciplines that make the work possible.

Purple Wombat draws on a network of specialist expertise across legal, financial and governance disciplines — all coordinated around community needs, with cultural safety at the centre.

Native Title & Trust Law

Deep understanding of the Native Title Act, consent determinations, ILUA structures, CATSI Act obligations, charitable trust law and ACNC compliance requirements — translated into plain language for communities.

Investment & Financial Governance

Investment Policy Statement development, asset allocation strategy, trustee oversight frameworks and investment performance review — all designed around the principle of perpetuity and community control.

Community Governance & Capacity

Board governance training, strategic planning, financial management, staffing structures and cultural governance integration — building the internal capacity of RNTBCs to lead their own futures.
